Four metres under Liisankatu

The cellar was cut in 1888 to keep a grain merchant’s stock cool through the summer. The walls are local granite, the ceiling is three brick vaults, and the temperature down here barely moves all year.

We put the fire where the loading hatch used to be, so the smoke goes straight up and out, and the room smells of birch rather than of cooking.
