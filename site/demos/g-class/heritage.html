From army truck to the longest-running Mercedes car.

Only the Unimog has been in Mercedes-Benz production longer.

  1. 1972

    Daimler-Benz and Steyr-Daimler-Puch agree to build a cross-country vehicle together. Stuttgart designs and tests it, Graz plans the production.

  2. 1979

    Production of the G model begins in Graz, almost entirely by hand. In some markets it is sold as the Puch G.

  3. 1989

    The German Bundeswehr picks the 250 GD as its light utility vehicle and calls it Wolf.

  4. 1990

    The W463 brings permanent four-wheel drive, wood trim and optional ABS to the civilian G.

  5. 1994

    The G model is officially renamed the G-Class.

  6. 2001

    The G-Class goes on sale in the United States.

  7. 2018

    A new generation, wider and with independent front suspension, keeps the shape, the hinges and the door sound.

  8. 2023

    The 500,000th G leaves Graz in agave green, a tribute to the jumping 240 GD in the 1979 press photo.

  9. 2024

    The G 580 with EQ Technology adds the first electric drivetrain.
